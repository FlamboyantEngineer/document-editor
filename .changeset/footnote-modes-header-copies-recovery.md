Footnotes are numbered in one mode for measuring, placing, editing and output, and their stored and page variables are laid out at the widths measured. A table's continuation records how many of its rows are header copies (`projectedHeaderRows`, a page-only attribute), so rows an author made of header cells stay through joins, selection, editing and numbering. Editable code keeps the caret row after a final newline and splits at blank lines with their own line numbers. A split paragraph keeps its number and its direction across its parts. A layout that waits or fails no longer settles early, a rejected layout transaction restores the page data, the pages are laid out again when the host's node view types change, fonts are asked for again for the current template, and the viewer follows the viewport when its scrolling frame changes.
