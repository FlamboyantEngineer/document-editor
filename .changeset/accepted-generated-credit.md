Generated text a person accepts is credited to them, still marked with its session: `decideSuggestions` and `decideStoredSuggestions` take a `decider`, and `decideWithPolicy` passes the policy's reader. An agent that names no session records the empty session `EMPTY_AI_SESSION`, so its text reads as generated. The room guard lets accepting re-credit only generated text, and only to the writer who accepts it.
