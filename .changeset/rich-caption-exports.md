Word and Markdown write a figure's or a table's caption in its formats, with its footnotes, comments and suggestions, and read it back. Word gives each kind of caption its own style, `Caption` for figures and `TableCaption` and `CodeCaption` based on it, with `TableCaptionLabel` and `CodeCaptionLabel` labels; the reader takes those labels off as it does `CaptionLabel`.
