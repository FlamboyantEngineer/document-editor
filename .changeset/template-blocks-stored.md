A template block now stores what its markup rendered as its own blocks, which the pages lay out and split like any other content, instead of a `renderedHtml` attribute drawn by a node view and measured as drawn. After any change to the stored texts or to a template block, the writer's own, another writer's, a server's or a replayed one, an editor that edits directly renders each block that holds anything else, as a derived change on nobody's undo stack, by deleting what it holds and inserting its render once it holds nothing; the rendered blocks take ids made from the block's id, markup and texts. Two editors who render at once in a room settle on one copy with whoever stays, and an editor that finds another editor's different render of the same markup and texts deletes it once, then leaves the room's and tells `onUnsettled` once (counted as `document-editor.template-block-unsettled`). The sync plugin sends local changes none of which is the writer's own, such as a change marked with `markDerived`, under its derived origin. A document opened with a block that holds anything else is rendered once its view opens. Typing, reading and suggesting render nothing. The writer cannot edit inside a template block, and Backspace and Delete beside one select it whole. `createDynamicTemplates` takes `{ enabled, onUnsettled }`; `renderTemplateBlock` gives the blocks as a ProseMirror fragment; `renderTemplateBlocks(tr, document)` renders a document's template blocks; `refreshDynamicTemplates` is removed. Markdown writes a template block's stored blocks. The stored `templateBlock` holds its blocks as `content`.
