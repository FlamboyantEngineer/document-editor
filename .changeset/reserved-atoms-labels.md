A citation's identifier with a character no served font draws takes its reserved box, and a mention, glossary reference, inline embed, footnote marker or template variable holding one is drawn in a box over the whole atom, so the engine and the page agree on its width. A caption's label that ends its line is drawn so though suggested text hidden at the caption's start comes first.
