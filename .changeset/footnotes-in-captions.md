A footnote may be cited in a figure's or table's caption, numbered in reading order with the text around it, and pasted markers are kept there; the caption a later part of a split table repeats still refuses one. `figure` joins the default citing places. While suggesting, a deletion that covers a whole figure suggests deleting it as one block, so accepting removes it whole; a whole table, which is no suggested block, has its text suggested for deletion as before.
