A suggester's typing on the pages reaches the room as one change that already carries the suggestion and its author, so the room's guard no longer refuses it and closes their connection. The pages' canonical state takes the transaction the dispatch body commits in place of the typed one (`PagedProjectionOptions.dispatch` may return `CanonicalTaken`). Document sessions expose `author`, and `DocumentSessionBoundary` passes it to the editor.
