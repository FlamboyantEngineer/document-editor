The last line of a justified top-level paragraph's part that continues on the next page is drawn justified and keeps its hyphen, in the editor, the viewer and print, as the whole paragraph would draw it, and is drawn again when a hard break typed at the top of the next part ends the paragraph's run there, or is taken out. A part inside a list, quote or other container keeps its last line ragged for now.
