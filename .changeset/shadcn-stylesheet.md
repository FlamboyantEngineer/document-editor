The package exports `./shadcn.css`, a shadcn/ui version of `./reference.css` to load after `@flamboyantengineer/ui-components/shadcn.css`: the same document and editor chrome partials, then a retouch that draws the editor's frame, bars, side panels and comment cards the shadcn/ui way, in the compact control scale. The pages keep the template's look. The labs offer it as the shadcn/ui stylesheet. The ui-components pin moves to fa30b68. In dark themes, the editor chrome's muted text now follows the theme, and the text style field keeps the chrome's text color instead of the template's paper color.
