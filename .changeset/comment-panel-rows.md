With the threads in the margin, the comments panel lists the open threads and standing suggestions as one row each under "In the document", naming who wrote it and the text it is on, rather than as a second card; activating a row puts the caret at its text and opens its card in the margin. Resolved threads keep their full cards in the panel, and `anchoredAs: 'cards'` keeps full cards for all of them. New labels `inDocument`, `threadRow`, `suggestionRow` and `onText`.
