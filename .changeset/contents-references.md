A list of references shows each cited document as its publisher, number with revision, a dash and
its title, a work with authors as a paper is cited, and a document the whole document formats by
that text; each revision cited is one entry. Its entries are grouped by reference type under
headings (`ContentsLabels.groups`), ordered by publisher and number, and a list shows only the
groups its block names (`contentsGroupsAt`, `setContentsGroups`, and a context menu submenu). An
entry for a citation opens where the host's `citationHref` says, in a new tab, or is plain text.
Kinds gain `group`, `groups`, `order` (with `compareOrder`) and `cites`.
