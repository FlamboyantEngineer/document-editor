A footnote drawn on more than one page, such as a note in a table's repeated header row, is numbered by the page each occurrence is on in the print drawn from the layout and in the viewer, and each page's marker links to that page's entry and back (`fnref-<id>~2`, `fn-<id>~2` on later pages) in those and in snapshots. The viewer reads a fragment with a literal `%` without an error and leaves modified, middle-button and cancelled clicks to the browser.
