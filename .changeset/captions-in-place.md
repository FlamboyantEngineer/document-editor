Table and figure captions are edited in the caption itself, with the clicks, double clicks and drags of any text; the text reaches the document on Enter or when focus leaves, and the session commits a caption being typed before it saves, flushes or merges (`holdPendingEdits`, `commitCaptionDraft`). The reference stylesheet keeps empty header and footer cells wide enough to click.
