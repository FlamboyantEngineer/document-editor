Comments show markers by default and open a card only on request: its marker, a click on its text,
the panel, a link or a new thread; opening focuses the card and a second click its reply box. A
primary click in the page puts a thread being started away, the same text may carry a further
thread, `openThread` centres both ways and keeps centring as the stage resizes, and `cardGap` sets
the gap beside the page (20 pixels by default).
