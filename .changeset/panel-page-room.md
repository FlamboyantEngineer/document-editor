A side panel leaves the document its page width at the zoom by default (`minMainSize`), stays collapsed while the page has no room beside it, and offers its resize handle only while expanded. Ctrl+K opens the toolbar's reference menu on its first entry while the toolbar shows it. `markupReasons` disables a markup choice with its reason. Neighbouring lists of one kind a local change leaves side by side join into one. The figure item is always a menu of the kinds of figure, and the inline completion toggle is disabled and not pressed in a read document. With the margin, `revealsPane` shows the comments pane for a thread started, opened or clicked. An inline completion offer goes when focus leaves the document, the document turns read-only, the provider turns unavailable or another writer changes its text block.

A deletion's comment marker stays by its line with change bars or no markup, where the deleted text is hidden, instead of falling away from it.
