Under `textLayout: 'pretext'`, the paragraphs and headings in a list's items are drawn on the lines the layout engine laid them out on with their list, in the editor, the viewer and prints, and `textLines()` gives them, marked `nested`. A split's empty placeholder at the start of a list item's continuation is left out.
