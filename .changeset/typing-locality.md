Typing that changes its block's height lays the pages out on the next frame, and typing that keeps every paragraph it reached at its height, unsplit, is not laid out at the pause. A text-only change walks no section it does not change (the script-output guard and table-width fitting read only the nodes a change reached). Manual page breaks take no page height and are drawn as a fading line; block embeds keep with the heading above them; template lengths read `pc` and `Q`.
