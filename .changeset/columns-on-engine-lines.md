Under `textLayout: 'pretext'`, the paragraphs and headings in a layout section's columns, and in lists inside them, are drawn on the engine's lines at their column's width. Typing in a figure's caption keeps the figure's measured image size, and a third-and-two-thirds section left with one column keeps that column's third, as the stylesheet draws it.
