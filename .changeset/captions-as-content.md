Table and figure captions are document content. A figure holds its caption (`figure_caption`) after its image, and a new node, `table_figure`, holds a table's caption (`table_caption`) and the table; the stored format keeps the table and writes each caption as the table's or figure's first child (`{ type: 'caption' }`), always, and no longer reads the `caption` and `captionId` attributes on tables and figures. A caption is edited as any text, in the document as it is typed and undone with the rest; `editCaption` selects its text, Select All in a caption selects its text first, and a press on a figure's image selects the figure. A code block's caption keeps its attribute and its field. `insertTable` and `insertFigure` give the object an empty caption, `deleteTableWithCaption` deletes a table with its caption, and `captionNodeAt` finds a table's or figure's caption node. The pages split a table with its caption, a later part showing the caption's text. Removed: `CAPTION_HOST_TYPES`, `commitCaptionDraft` and the editor's held caption commit. The reference stylesheet places a table's caption with `--doc-table-caption-order`, which replaces `--doc-table-caption-side`. A room's guard refuses a caption showing text only the pages draw, and reads a change exactly as the room holds it, so content the schema refuses where it is, such as a table outside its holder, is refused instead of fitted in.
