The context menu stays open and moves with its content as the document scrolls, ends with a `paragraphs` group (add a paragraph before or after an object, script blocks included, now kind `script`) and offers Copy link (`onCopyLink`, Mod-Shift-K). The View menu no longer lists layouts. The side panel takes `minMainSize` and always shows its comments pane, with an unavailable state when there are none, and the mention menu shows each person's avatar.
