Session in a room

Two readers open the same document in its shared room, on a room server in this page. What one types reaches the other, and the room stores it. Take a reader offline to keep their changes until they come back online. The room's write guard holds each reader to the rights set below; let the editor stop following them to see the guard refuse a change. The room keeps its threads, and the room controls make changes that do not merge, break the guard's self-check or write what the editors cannot read. Reload the page to start over.
