Contents

The table of contents and the table of tables list the document's headings and tables, numbered as the document numbers them, with the page each one is on. The table of contents continues on the next page; its levels nest down to level 6, each in the type the template gives it, and an entry too long for its row wraps. Insert a chapter and every number after it changes; select an entry to go to what it names. The table of quotations at the end is a kind of list the lab configures itself: any node type can be listed. The list of references lists each cited document and revision once, by publisher and number, under the heading of its kind of document; a document the host gives an address opens in a new tab.
