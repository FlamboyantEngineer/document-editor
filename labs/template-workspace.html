Template workspace

Open a template from its folder, edit its document and section tabs, and save it. The side panel holds the Properties and History. Save elsewhere stands for another writer saving the same template, so your next save asks you to reload or keep your changes. The Article template is for the web layout: it inherits nothing, keeps its name, and has its margins in pixels. Show the stored template shows the open template as the store holds it. The host options let the host hold the template and version as a route, picked with its own template picker; start at the first version; draw its own preview or lay the parts out in its own frame; start the side panel expanded; and remember the pane under its own key.
